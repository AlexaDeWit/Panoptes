# Support

Where to take a question, a bug, or a security report. I maintain Panoptes in
spare time and it's pre-1.0, so please help me keep the signal high.

- **Start with the docs.** [`README.md`](README.md) covers what Panoptes is
  and how to build it. [`CONTRIBUTING.md`](CONTRIBUTING.md) covers the
  contribution process.
- **Found a bug or want a capability?** Open an
  [issue](https://github.com/AlexaDeWit/Panoptes/issues/new).
- **Security vulnerability?** Do not open a public issue. Follow
  [`SECURITY.md`](SECURITY.md) for private reporting.

There's no SLA. I answer well-scoped, reproducible reports fastest.
